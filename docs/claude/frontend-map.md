# Frontend catalog

Moved verbatim from the old CLAUDE.md (lines 244-288). Not loaded at session start: read it on demand, only when the task touches this area.

- **Frontend** (`frontend/`): React 19, react-router 7, Vite 8, TypeScript. No chart library since UX-16 (D-648, D-657: `recharts` and its peer `react-is` removed): the charts are HTML and CSS. No state library: pages load with `useApi` and write with `useMutation`. `components.tsx`'s `ToggleGroup` (UX-1) is a labelled `role="group"` of `aria-pressed` buttons that wrap.
  - `main.tsx` asks `/api/me` first (8 seconds at most). Without a session, `/` shows `Landing.tsx` and every other path goes to the login; with the backend down or not answering, `/` shows the landing page too. `Landing.tsx` needs nothing from the backend (WEB-1, D-979 to D-981: on the apex host, `isApexHost` in `links.ts`, `startApex` in `apex.tsx` runs first and never asks `/api/me`: with the cookie `ft_session=1` (`hint.ts`, set by the app on a valid `/api/me`, removed on 401, 403 and logout; `Domain` the parent like `ft_theme`) it goes to the app, else it shows the landing page with its sign-in links at app.finance-nl.com; both footers end with the author's link, `AUTHOR_URL`): what the app is, how to try it, how it's built, two screenshots of the demo (`src/assets/demo-*.webp`, taken from a local instance), and plain links to the login, the source and `/privacy`. `links.ts` holds those addresses.
  - `public/privacy.html` (with `privacy.css`): the privacy policy, static HTML without script, served at `/privacy` by nginx (`$uri.html`) and by the Vite servers (the `staticPages` plugin in `vite.config.ts`). The owner finalized it on 2026-09-28. Since F8c (last updated 7 October 2026) it names the time zone among the settings it stores; since UX-9 (last updated 11 November 2026) both family lists name the payee a member chooses to show; since F6c it has the section "Family budgets" (`#family`, ahead of their launch), word for word the text of `docs/family-budget/privacy-draft.md` that the owner reviewed on 2026-10-02 with F6c's fixes (`privacy.test.ts` compares them).
  - `public/favicon.svg` and `favicon.ico` (the same icon as PNGs of 16 and 32 px): the icon of the app's pages and of `/privacy`.
  - `api.ts` calls `/api/*` with the session cookie and `X-XSRF-TOKEN`; a 401 sends the browser to `/oauth2/authorization/keycloak`. It holds the API's types. A failed call throws `ApiError`, which carries the problem's `errors` (400) and `violations` (422); `useApi` also returns the `status` of a failed load. `Me.features.familyLedgers` is the family switch; `familyLedgersOn(me)` reads it, and a missing field counts as off. `useApi` takes an optional `guard` (F8c, `guards.ts`: `isMe`, `isBalances`, `isReport`, `isRecordPage`, the shapes whose nested fields the screens read): an answer it refuses is the error `UNEXPECTED_ANSWER`, never data. `Safe` (`components.tsx`, an `ErrorBoundary` with a short "Couldn’t show …" and a reload link, reset by the address) stands around the app's routes (`App.tsx`), around a family budget's tabs (`Family.tsx`) and around the overview's widgets (the reader's balance, the latest activity), so that a page that throws in render leaves the header, the budget's title and tabs and the other widgets, never a blank page. `main.tsx` shows a notice for a `/api/me` that isn't `isMe`, which needs only `today` as a date ("2026-10-07") and takes `name`, `email` and `timeZone` as a string, null or missing and `features` as an object, null or missing (F8c-fix: an account registered with its email only has `name` null and works; its header shows the email and a new family budget asks for a name).
  - Today and the user's time zone (F8c, D-100, D-101): `me.tsx`'s `MeProvider` (inside `App`) holds `/api/me` (`name`, null for an account with an email only, `email`, `timeZone`, `today`), and `useToday()` is every screen's today (new entry's date, a family record's and settlement's date, a budget's start date, a claim's join date, the rates form, the dashboard's and the entry list's periods, via `dateOfIso`); no screen reads the browser's clock for a date. The first time it sees no zone it PUTs `/api/settings/time-zone` with the browser's (`Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone`, once; any failure, 422, 5xx or network, leaves the zone unset (UTC) with no error and no retry before the next page load, D-103), and asks `/api/me` again when the tab becomes visible (its answer older than a minute), once at the next midnight of the saved zone (one timer, armed again after each answer, `msUntilMidnight`) and after "Delete all my data" (the midnight request is not sent while the tab is hidden: it notes that it missed the date and the visibility handler asks when the tab is shown, D-107); nothing polls, since a background poll would keep the BFF and SSO sessions alive for ever (D-106). `TimeZone.tsx`: `ZoneHint` ("Your browser is in X, and dates here follow Y", `Use X` or Dismiss, kept in `localStorage` for that pair of zones; only for a zone in GET `/api/settings/time-zones`, the ids the api accepts, D-103) and `TimeZoneSettings` on the Settings page (the zone, today there, a search box and a list of `Intl.supportedValuesOf('timeZone')` and UTC, kept to the accepted ones once that list has been asked for, `timeZone.ts`). The tests run in TZ=UTC (`vite.config.ts`), and a page rendered without the app takes `WithMe` (`testMe.tsx`).
  - `money.ts`: amounts are decimal strings, as the API sends them. All arithmetic goes through big.js in strict mode, never JS numbers, rounding HALF_UP. `formatMoney` formats the string with `Intl.NumberFormat` in the currency. `toChartNumber` is the one conversion to a JS number, for bar heights only.
