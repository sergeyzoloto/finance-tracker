Finance Tracker

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Finance Tracker, at app.finance-nl.com, is a personal, non-commercial portfolio project. This page explains what personal data it processes, why, for how long, and what your rights are. It is written for the app and for the login service auth.finance-nl.com that the app uses.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Sergey Zolotko, Rotterdam, the Netherlands. For any question or request about your data, email serge@finance-nl.com.

What data is processed

Your login account

Your account is held by the login service, auth.finance-nl.com. It stores your email address, your name, a hash of your password if you set one, and whether your email address is verified. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, it also stores your user id at that provider (for GitHub, your username) and the name and email address the provider shares.

When you sign in, the app receives your user id at the login service, your email address and your name, and stores them, so that it knows which data is yours.

What you put into the app

Everything you enter in the app, import into it or load as demo data: accounts, categories, counterparties (such as the shops you pay or a friend you lend money to), entries with their dates, amounts and notes, exchange rates you enter, and your settings. Your settings include your time zone, such as Europe/Amsterdam, which the app takes from your browser the first time you use it and which you can change in Settings: it decides which date is “today” for you, for example the date a new entry or a family budget starts on. No other user of the app can see it, apart from what you record in a family budget, which its members see (Family budgets). As the operator, I have technical access to the database and its backups, and I use it only to run the service, to fix a problem you report, or when the law requires it. If you write down other people’s names, for example as someone you lent money to, they are part of your data and not visible to other users.

When you import a spreadsheet, the app reads the uploaded files while it handles the upload and does not keep them. It keeps what the import created (entries, accounts, categories and counterparties) and a record of the import: the name of the transactions file, a checksum of its content, when the import ran, and how many entries, errors and warnings it had. A trial run (“dry run”) and a failed import keep nothing.

The demo data is invented and is not about any real person.

Technical data

When you use the app or the login service, the web server and the login server log your IP address and the details of your requests. The login service also records login and account events, such as signing in or changing a password, and administrative events.

Family budgets

A family budget is a budget you keep with other people, such as a partner or your children. You can belong to several. Each member keeps their own personal budget, which stays private. What the family spends and earns together is recorded in the family budget, and each member’s share of it appears in their personal budget.

What other members of a family budget see

Everyone who is a member of a family budget sees, for that budget:

  • every member’s display name: the name you choose when you join, which you can change at any time, and the names given to members without an account;
  • the family records: expenses, incomes and settlements between members, with their dates, amounts (and the original amount and currency, with the exchange rate used), who paid or received them, and each member’s share;
  • the family categories, such as “Groceries”;
  • the family comments written on records;
  • each member’s balance in the family budget, and who owes whom;
  • the family report: the family’s expenses and incomes by month and category, with each member’s share and what they paid or received;
  • the change journal: who added, changed or deleted which record, when, and what the values were before and after.

What other members never see

Other members never see anything of your personal budget:

  • your accounts, such as which card you paid with, and their balances;
  • your personal categories;
  • your personal entries, including the entries a family budget adds to your personal budget;
  • your private notes on your own payments;
  • your email address and your account id.

Invite links

An owner of a family budget invites people with a link. Whoever holds a link can use it once, and only before it expires (after 72 hours, unless the owner chose a shorter or longer time of up to 7 days). The owner can revoke a link before it is used. Keep a link you receive to yourself.

Before you accept or decline, the invitation shows you:

  • the family budget’s name and currency;
  • the display name of the owner who invites you;
  • whether you join as a new member or take the place of a member without an account, and that member’s name;
  • the date from which the family budget’s records are added to your personal budget, and until when the link works;
  • for a place, its balance in the family budget before that date, which becomes your opening balance;
  • if you were a member before and come back, the one correction that brings what your personal budget shows as your balance with the family budget in line with the family budget’s;
  • the family budget’s categories;
  • which of your own categories will merge into the family budget’s, which stay private, and which you may bring along;
  • if you come back, the entries of your own on your earlier balance with the family budget that are dated after the day you come back, with their dates, amounts and notes: you move or delete them before you accept;
  • the name of your account, as a suggestion for the name the other members will see.

Nothing else about the family budget or its members is shown before you accept: no member’s name but the owner’s and the place’s, and no amount of the family budget’s but that opening balance or correction.

The secret part of the link is never sent to our server with the address of a page, so it doesn’t appear in server logs. We store only a hash of it, from which the link can’t be rebuilt. To show you the invitation, you have to sign in first.

When you accept, the owners see your display name and when you accepted. When you decline, they see only that the invite was declined, and when; nothing about you is stored.

Members without an account

A family budget can include people who don’t use Finance Tracker, such as a child. Their names are entered by the budget’s owners, and other members record expenses, incomes and settlements for them. If you enter someone’s name, enter only what they would be happy for the other members to see: a first name or a nickname is enough.

Such a person can later be invited to take their place in the budget. From the date chosen in the invite, their shares then appear in their own personal budget, and their balance before that date arrives as one opening balance. They keep their place in the records of before that date, which can still be changed, and such a change moves their opening balance.

Leaving, removal and “Delete all my data”

When you leave a family budget, or an owner removes you:

  • you no longer see the family budget, and nothing more is added to your personal budget;
  • what was already added stays in your personal budget as your own entries, which you can change or delete; the family categories they use become personal categories of yours, with the names they have at that moment; your balance with the family budget stays in your personal budget as an account of yours;
  • the family records stay in the family budget, because they make up the other members’ balances. The other members keep seeing your display name in them, marked as a member who left, and the records that involve you can no longer be changed;
  • if the family budget split its expenses by fixed percentages and you had a share, it goes back to equal shares, and its change journal says that this happened when you left;
  • invite links you created that weren’t used yet stop working.

The last owner of a family budget hands ownership to another member first. When the last member with an account leaves, the family budget is deleted, with its records; the confirmation says so before.

If an owner invites you again, you come back in your earlier place, from the day you accept. The family budget’s categories you use become the family’s again, and one correction on that day brings your balance with the family budget in your personal budget in line with the family budget’s: for example, if you changed or deleted entries it had added, or records of before you left changed while you were away. The invitation shows the correction before you accept. Entries of your own on that balance dated after the day you come back aren’t part of the family budget: the invitation lists them, and you move or delete them first.

Before you confirm “Delete all my data”, the screen lists every family budget you are in: your role and balance there, and what will happen to it, such as which member becomes its owner, or that it will be deleted. Of family budgets you left, it says only how many there are.

When you use “Delete all my data”, your personal budget is deleted entirely. In each family budget:

  • the records that affect other members’ balances stay, with their amounts, dates, categories and shares, but your name in them is replaced by “Former member”;
  • the comments you wrote are erased, also from the change journal;
  • invite links you created and that weren’t used yet stop working;
  • if it split its expenses by fixed percentages and you had a share, it goes back to equal shares;
  • if you were its last owner, the member with an account who joined earliest becomes its owner;
  • if no other member with an account is left, the family budget is deleted.

Your login account at auth.finance-nl.com stays until you ask us to delete it, as the published policy says.

How long family records are kept

The records of a family budget are kept as long as the family budget exists, also after a member leaves or deletes their data, since they make up the other members’ balances. A family budget is deleted when no member with an account is left in it, and with it its records, its categories and its change journal.

The demo family budget

Once family budgets are available, the demo data comes with a family budget, “Demo household”, which you share with Sam, an invented partner without an account. Nobody else is in it, and “Delete all my data” deletes it with the rest.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the service — your account, signing you in, and storing and showing your data: this is necessary to provide the service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Security and abuse prevention, through the logs: this is my legitimate interest in keeping the service and your data safe (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your data is not used for anything else. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Cookies

Only cookies that are strictly necessary for the service are used:

  • the app’s session cookie (JSESSIONID), which keeps you signed in, and its XSRF-TOKEN cookie, which protects your changes against forged requests from other sites. Both end when you close your browser; on the server, the session ends when you sign out or after 30 minutes without activity;
  • the login service’s session cookies, which keep you signed in there.

While you sign in, the app also remembers in your browser’s session storage which page to take you back to, and after an update of the app it notes there when it reloaded the page, so that it reloads only once. Because all of this is strictly necessary, the app doesn’t ask for your consent.

Who else processes your data

  • Hetzner Online GmbH hosts the app, the login service and their databases, in its data centre in Falkenstein, Germany.
  • Brevo sends the login service’s account emails, such as email verification and password reset.
  • Google or GitHub, only if you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply to what they process.

Transfers outside the EU

The app, the login service, their databases and all backups are stored in the EU. Brevo stores its data in the EU, but states that companies of its group and its sub-processors may access it from outside the EU, in particular from the United States and India; Brevo protects such transfers with the safeguards the GDPR requires, as described in its own privacy policy. If you choose to sign in with Google or GitHub, these companies are based in the United States and process your data under their own privacy policies. Nothing else is transferred outside the EU.

How long it is kept

  • Your account and your data are kept until you delete them or ask for your account to be deleted. Delete all my data, in the app’s Settings, deletes everything you have in the app immediately. Your login account stays until you ask for it to be deleted by email.
  • The records of a family budget are kept as long as the family budget exists, also after you leave it or delete your data (How long family records are kept).
  • Login and account events are kept for 30 days, administrative events for 90 days.
  • The logs of the web server and of the login server are rotated by size, not by time, so on this small server they can be kept for several months.
  • Nightly database backups are kept for 14 days on the server and for up to 60 days on my own computer, so deleted data disappears from the backups within 60 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you;
  • have it corrected: you can change your profile data yourself in the login service’s account console, and everything in your ledger in the app;
  • have it erased: “Delete all my data” erases your data in the app at once, and your login account is deleted on request;
  • restrict its processing;
  • receive it in a portable, machine-readable form (data portability);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To use any of these rights, email serge@finance-nl.com. You will get an answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Automated decisions

The service makes no automated decisions about you and does no profiling.

What you need to provide

To use the app you need an account: an email address and a password, or a Google or GitHub account. Without one, the app can’t be used. The landing page and this policy can be read without an account.

Children
