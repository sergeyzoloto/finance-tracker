Finance Tracker

Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Finance Tracker, at app.finance-nl.com, is a personal, non-commercial portfolio project. This page explains what personal data it processes, why, for how long, and what your rights are. It is written for the app and for the login service auth.finance-nl.com that the app uses.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Sergey Zolotko, Rotterdam, the Netherlands. For any question or request about your data, email serge@finance-nl.com.

What data is processed

Your login account

Your account is held by the login service, auth.finance-nl.com. It stores your email address, your name, a hash of your password if you set one, and whether your email address is verified. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, it also stores your user id at that provider (for GitHub, your username) and the name and email address the provider shares.

When you sign in, the app receives your user id at the login service, your email address and your name, and stores them, so that it knows which data is yours.

What you put into the app

Everything you enter in the app, import into it or load as demo data: accounts, categories, counterparties (such as the shops you pay or a friend you lend money to), entries with their dates, amounts and notes, exchange rates you enter, and your settings. Only you can see it. If you write down other people’s names, for example as someone you lent money to, they are part of your data and visible only to you.

When you import a spreadsheet, the app reads the uploaded files while it handles the upload and does not keep them. It keeps what the import created (entries, accounts, categories and counterparties) and a record of the import: the name of the transactions file, a checksum of its content, when the import ran, and how many entries, errors and warnings it had. A trial run (“dry run”) and a failed import keep nothing.

The demo data is invented and is not about any real person.

Technical data

When you use the app or the login service, the web server and the login server log your IP address and the details of your requests. The login service also records login and account events, such as signing in or changing a password, and administrative events.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the service — your account, signing you in, and storing and showing your data: this is necessary to provide the service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Security and abuse prevention, through the logs: this is my legitimate interest in keeping the service and your data safe (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Your data is not used for anything else. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Cookies

Only cookies that are strictly necessary for the service are used:

  • the app’s session cookie (JSESSIONID), which keeps you signed in, and its XSRF-TOKEN cookie, which protects your changes against forged requests from other sites. Both end when you close your browser; on the server, the session ends when you sign out or after 30 minutes without activity;
  • the login service’s session cookies, which keep you signed in there.

While you sign in, the app also remembers in your browser’s session storage which page to take you back to, and after an update of the app it notes there when it reloaded the page, so that it reloads only once. Because all of this is strictly necessary, the app doesn’t ask for your consent.

Who else processes your data

  • Hetzner Online GmbH hosts the app, the login service and their databases, in its data centre in Falkenstein, Germany.
  • Brevo sends the login service’s account emails, such as email verification and password reset.
  • Google or GitHub, only if you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply to what they process.

All app data is stored in the EU.

How long it is kept

  • Your account and your data are kept until you delete them or ask for your account to be deleted. Delete all my data, in the app’s Settings, deletes everything you have in the app immediately. Your login account stays until you ask for it to be deleted by email.
  • Login and account events are kept for 30 days, administrative events for 90 days.
  • The web server’s logs are rotated by size, not by time, so on this small server they can be kept for several months.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you;
  • have it corrected: you can change your profile data yourself in the login service’s account console, and everything in your ledger in the app;
  • have it erased: “Delete all my data” erases your data in the app at once, and your login account is deleted on request;
  • restrict its processing;
  • receive it in a portable, machine-readable form (data portability);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To use any of these rights, email serge@finance-nl.com. You will get an answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 16.

Security

The app and the login service are served only over HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as hashes, and only I have access to the server.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
